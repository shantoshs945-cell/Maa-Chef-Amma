Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At Maa Chef Amma, we take great pride in the quality of our homemade products. Since our products are food items prepared fresh in small batches, we have a limited but fair refund and cancellation policy. Please read this carefully before placing an order.

Refund Eligibility

We offer refunds or replacements in the following cases:

✅
Full Refund
Wrong product delivered or item missing from order
✅
Full Refund
Product damaged or leaking upon delivery
⚡
Replacement
Spoiled or expired product received
❌
No Refund
Change of mind after order is dispatched
❌
No Refund
Product opened and partially consumed
❌
No Refund
Incorrect address provided by customer

Cancellation Policy

  • Orders can be cancelled within 2 hours of placing the order.
  • To cancel, contact us immediately via WhatsApp at +91 99893 40476.
  • Once the order has been packed or dispatched, cancellation is not possible.
  • If cancellation is approved before dispatch, a full refund will be processed within 5–7 business days.

⏱️ Cancellation window: 2 hours from order placement.
After this window, we begin preparing and packing your order and cancellations cannot be accepted.

Refund Timeline

Once a refund is approved, the amount will be credited back to the original payment method within:

  • UPI / PhonePe / GPay: 3–5 business days
  • Bank Transfer: 5–7 business days

Refund processing times may vary depending on your bank or UPI provider.

How to Raise a Refund Request

To raise a refund or replacement request, follow these steps:

1
Contact us within 48 hours of receiving your order via WhatsApp at +91 99893 40476.
2
Share your order details — order ID, name, and phone number used while ordering.
3
Send clear photos or videos of the damaged, wrong, or defective product.
4
Our team will review your request within 24–48 hours and respond with the resolution.
5
Refund or replacement will be processed once the request is approved.

Important Notes

  • Refund requests raised after 48 hours of delivery will not be entertained.
  • We reserve the right to reject refund requests that do not meet the above criteria.
  • Maa Chef Amma is not responsible for delays in delivery caused by courier partners or weather conditions.
  • In case of non-delivery, please contact us within 10 days of the expected delivery date.
